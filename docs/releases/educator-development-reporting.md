# Reports an educator can act on

The opening report now answers who learners were at the start, what changed, where practical support is needed, and what to do next. Authored capability names remain intact and have everyday explanations. Learner accounts, assessed tasks, repeated use, follow-up and use in another setting retain their separate meanings.

Learners see their work and next practice. Facilitators see a support priority and strengths before opening evidence details. Schools see intervention planning and a group heatmap. Partners see development outcomes and what these results can and cannot establish about BIS. Detailed charts are available under “How we reached these conclusions”; the Evidence section opens that report directly. The learning comparison action now leads there.

The public Habit / Habit + Decision example uses these shared components and calculations. The PDF starts with a plain-language development summary before its evidence appendix. The example remains fictional and does not write programme evidence. Existing Leap9 experiences are unchanged.

Verification covers reconciliation of the narrative with source counts; mixed, missing and self-reported evidence; lower later results; all four audiences; keyboard disclosures; switching, Back, refresh, decisions, privacy, PDF geometry and mobile overflow. The release requires the complete `npm run verify` contract on the exact PR head.

Limits: clearer reporting does not validate the measurement model or prove causation. Actual programmes still need approved task criteria and reviewed evidence. Educator usability should continue to be assessed with real readers; passing automated checks alone does not establish comprehension.

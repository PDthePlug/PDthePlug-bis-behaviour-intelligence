# Profile and workspace improvements

The 34 component/section families in `profile-workspace-component-register.json` cover account Profile, Settings, My experience, installation, Evidence Portfolio, result history, handbook/Lab profiles, both Leap9 demonstrations, every facilitator/sponsor/operator section, safeguarding, Content Studio and every Commercial workspace section. The route and edition registers retain the remaining exact variants. These suggestions do not declare certification complete.
